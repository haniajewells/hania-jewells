HERE TO HELP

Frequently asked questions

What kind of jewellery does Hania Jewels offer?

Hania Jewels focuses on casual, everyday fashion jewellery such as pearls, beads, necklaces, bracelets, anklets, rings, earrings, chains and charms. We do not feature heavy bridal or wedding sets.

Can I customize every product?

Customization is offered on selected necklaces, bracelets and anklets. Other products have a details view so you can review their description and price before adding them to your bag.

How do I choose my Karachi location?

Select your area on the location screen before entering the shop. The previously selected area is pre-filled when you visit again, and you can change it before pressing Continue.

How can I place an order?

Add items to your bag and continue to checkout. Enter your valid contact and delivery details, then continue to WhatsApp. Press Send in WhatsApp to submit your order to Hania Jewels.

Are online payments available?

Online card payments are not available at the moment. Once your order is sent on WhatsApp, Hania Jewels will confirm the total, delivery charges and payment method with you.

How can I contact Hania Jewels?

Contact details will be added once the store's official phone number, email and social links are provided.
