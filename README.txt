HANIA JEWELS - WEBSITE FILES

1. Extract the ZIP folder and open index.html in Chrome to preview the website.
2. Upload the complete hania_jewels folder to a GitHub repository and enable GitHub Pages to publish it.
3. A two-second Hania Jewels brand intro appears first on every visit. After it, visitors must select a Karachi area and press Continue to enter the store.
4. The location selector groups Karachi localities under the seven administrative districts. If a smaller locality is not listed, visitors can choose “My Karachi area is not listed” and type it.
5. Product photo areas are intentionally blank until you provide the product images.
6. Includes 30 products, category cards, separate product details/customization popups, cart, wishlist, offers, reviews and FAQ.
7. Checkout validates the customer's name, email, Pakistani mobile number and delivery address. It then opens WhatsApp with a pre-filled order message addressed to the Hania Jewels business number configured in script.js.
8. The customer must press Send in WhatsApp for the order to reach you. The website cannot detect whether the customer actually sent the message, and it does not store orders on a server. The cart is kept until the customer clears it.
9. Online payments are not connected. Confirm delivery charges, availability and payment details with the customer on WhatsApp.
10. The business WhatsApp number is set near the top of script.js as WHATSAPP_BUSINESS_NUMBER. It is currently configured as 923392117615.
11. Location, cart, wishlist and reviews are saved in the visitor's browser using localStorage. The selected location is pre-filled on later visits, but visitors must still press Continue after the intro to enter the store.
12. This version uses a single light theme; the dark-mode toggle and dark-mode styling have been removed.
13. Animated version: animations.css and animations.js add scroll reveals, floating sparkles, a scrolling category ribbon, shimmering headings, shiny buttons, heart pops and badge bumps. They respect 'reduce motion' settings.
14. Price caps: Necklaces under Rs. 1,000, Bracelets under Rs. 500, Anklets and Earrings under Rs. 300, Rings / Charms / Pearls under Rs. 300, Chains under Rs. 500.
15. Internet is required for Bootstrap and Google Fonts.
